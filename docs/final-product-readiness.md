## Judge journey

1. Open the live product in a fresh private window. Choose **Open judge
   workspace** to inspect the completed record or **Try a fresh handover** to
   perform the workflow. No model key or shared password is needed.
2. Inspect a knowledge record and its exact source/version/location. A candidate
   is not verified merely because it was extracted.
3. In a fresh workspace, run Investigation and review supported candidates.
   Generate and approve a handover as Rudransh.
4. Propose responsibilities. Switch to Savinay to accept or reject and submit
   cited practical work. Switch back for separate supervisor assessment.
5. Inspect **Before you close this handover**. Acceptance, supervisor-reviewed
   tasks, evidence coverage and current blockers are visible before closure.
   Missing or failed readiness disables Close; the backend validates again.
6. Follow the access, source, practical-review or prerequisite links to resolve
   incomplete work. Changed evidence requires revalidation; old completion is
   historical rather than automatically current.
7. Review required checks and unresolved follow-up owners, then close only when
   authorised checks pass. Inspect Savinay's continuation plan and original
   ownership provenance.

## Demonstrate the three promised improvements

- **Onboarding/access:** create a one-time invitation; recipient sets their own
  password. Inspect a missing source permission, submit an access request, review
  it independently, then revoke membership and verify denial. Operational IAM is
  separate from INHERIA knowledge access.
- **Investigation/change:** replace a document, inspect downstream impact, see old
  claims lose authority, then investigate and review the new version. The five
  checkpoint interruption tests and actual local process restart record prove
  bounded recovery without claiming a deliberate live outage.
- **Practical recovery:** reject incomplete ownership or request correction of an
  exercise, resubmit and reassess. Source-version changes, missing required
  prerequisites or revoked evidence prevent closure. A runbook is not evidence
  that a staging restore succeeded.
