# Workspace completion and contributions

The project shell displays completed knowledge transfer on every signed-in page.
The banner links to the exact handover version; overview lists recent handovers
and the team page shows each participant's recorded contributions. This applies
to the selected project, preserving other projects and their independent state.

The summary is derived from persisted, permission-filtered evidence and workflow
records, rather than seeded success labels. It counts accepted responsibilities,
reviewed walkthroughs, interview responses, knowledge reviews and ownership
transfers. Project/source permissions apply before aggregation. A missing or
deleted source suppresses dependent completion; a closed version that no longer
passes readiness is labelled as requiring revalidation. A failed summary request
has a retry state.

The ShopEase account contains an author-operated Gaurav-to-Savinay handover, with
Rudransh coordinating review. Its app workflow is complete: eight distinct
responsibilities accepted and eleven knowledge walkthroughs reviewed. The case
study does not establish real Wipro operations, independent human assessment,
successful cloud deployments or restore tests. Open work and missing external
recovery evidence remain visible and require their own follow-up.

Verification covers completion before/after closure, successor visibility,
cross-tenant denial, restricted contribution counts, source deletion, all twelve
workspace routes, exact handover navigation, participant cards and day/night
desktop/mobile accessibility. See verification.md for executed results.
